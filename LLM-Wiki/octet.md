# octet

## X-ray micro-CT dimensional accuracy and manufacturability assessment
- last_updated: 2026-07-29
- source_doi: 10.1115/1.0004182v

Register the reconstructed CT volume to the nominal octet-truss geometry, segment solid material, and quantify deviations in strut dimensions, node/junction geometry, and overall manufacturability. Use the resulting dimensional-error distributions as core EDA features.

## CT segmentation and quantitative pore/solid morphology analysis
- last_updated: 2026-07-29
- source_doi: 10.1029/2009wr008087

Apply validated thresholding or related segmentation to separate the lattice solid from void space, then quantify solid volume fraction, pore/void volume, pore-size or opening distributions, and microcrack-like discontinuities. Treat segmentation sensitivity and image resolution as explicit quality-control variables.

## X-ray CT wall-thickness and through-hole defect evaluation
- last_updated: 2026-07-29
- source_doi: 10.1007/s10921-025-01269-8

Measure local wall/strut thickness throughout the reconstructed lattice and detect blocked or undersized through-holes. Summarize spatial defect maps, thickness statistics, and defect counts for manufacturing-quality EDA.

## 3D pore connectivity and anisotropic tortuosity analysis
- last_updated: 2026-07-29
- source_doi: 10.1080/18811248.2007.9711367

Build a 3D void-space connectivity representation from the segmented CT volume and calculate connected-component structure, directional connectivity, and anisotropic tortuosity. These features characterize whether the octet lattice's open-cell pathways are continuous and directionally biased.

## Octet-truss strut-geometry morphometrics
- last_updated: 2026-07-29
- source_doi: 10.20528/cjsmec.2023.04.002

Extract strut length, radius/diameter, cross-sectional variation, node/fillet geometry, and their distributions from the CT reconstruction. Preserve orientation-specific statistics because octet-truss effective behavior is linked to strut geometry and loading direction.
